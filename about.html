How It All Began ...

Our Story

The Vigilant Heart Charitable Society (VHCS) was incorporated and formally registered as a nongovernmental and non-profit charitable trust under the Corporate Affairs Commission of the Federal Republic of Nigeria in September 2007. Before then, Mrs. Maria Iriowen Osawemen, with an uncommon passion for helping the needy and underprivileged, had been engaged actively in works of charity at various streets, centres and orphanages in Lagos State. She recognizes and reveres Christ in the underprivileged. Initially, the idea of establishing an orphanage never occurred to her, but the call and determination to serve orphans and the disadvantaged was her preoccupation. She visited orphanages, fed and bathed the children, and supplied some of their basic necessities.

Filled with the inspiration to render services and provide adequate care for motherless babies, orphans, and abandoned children, she decided to organise a support group. She set up a Mobile Soup Kitchen and invited friends and volunteers who included wives of members of the diplomatic corps that accompanied her in many of her visits to Rehabilitation and Training Centre (RTC), Majidun, Ikorodu, the Remand Home, Girls’ Approved School, and transit Village for motherless children, Idi-Araba, all in Lagos State. The services of the Mobile Soup Kitchen was also extended to children living in other disadvantaged communities.

Mrs. Maria Osawemen's passion for the underprivileged, sympathy for the suffering, and her self-sacrificing charity was given a form and structure when The Vigilant Heart Charitable Society was formally registered by Barr. Osawemen as a non-profit organisation for the provision of shelter for orphans, abandoned, and vulnerable children. It was also established to foster access to good quality education by underprivileged children and protect children against abuses at every stage of their development.

Purpose & Direction

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To create a safe and caring environment for the comprehensive development of orphans and abandoned children, promote formal education for indigent and underprivileged children as well as work on projects that protect children against numerous ills and vicissitudes in the society.

Our Core Values

Hard work, honesty, piety and kindness. Although VHCS is motivated by Christian principles and ideals, it does not consider religion, environmental or cultural backgrounds in carrying out its work of charity.

Answers You Need

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How can I support the children financially?

    You can make a direct bank transfer to any of our dedicated accounts (Zenith Bank, Globus Bank, or EcoBank). Simply click the "Donate" button located at the top of our website for complete details and account numbers.

  • How does the child sponsorship program work?

    Child sponsorship is one of the most personal ways you can help. You can choose to cover a child's education, their daily meals, or their healthcare needs. Visit our 'Sponsor a Child' page to learn how you can start changing a life today.

  • Are visitors allowed at the orphanage?

    Absolutely! We always welcome kind-hearted visitors. Spending time with the children means the world to them. We just ask that you contact us in advance to schedule your visit so our staff can properly welcome you.

  • Exactly how are my donations utilized?

    Every single naira you give goes straight toward the welfare of our children. This covers their daily nutritious meals, school fees and supplies, routine healthcare, and keeping our home environment safe and well-maintained.

  • I want to volunteer my time. How do I start?

    We are always thrilled to welcome passionate volunteers to the VHCS family. Whether you want to tutor, help with administration, or organize weekend activities, there's a place for you here. Use the 'Volunteer' button to get in touch!

  • Is Vigilant Heart a registered entity?

    Yes. The Vigilant Heart Charitable Society (VHCS) is fully registered and recognized as a non-governmental, non-profit organization by the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

  • How can I reach out to the management?

    You can easily reach us via email at info@vigilantheartsociety.org, or by calling our office line at +234 803 718 3205, +234 805 030 1262. We'd love to hear from you.
